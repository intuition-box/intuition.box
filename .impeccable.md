## Design Context

### Users

Intuition Learn serves builders who are new to the protocol, from absolute beginners learning the graph model to React and TypeScript developers preparing a first Testnet integration. They arrive to understand atoms, triples, signal, and the safe path from an app idea to a protocol-backed product.

### Brand Personality

Calm, credible, and protocol-native. The experience should build confidence through precise language, progressive disclosure, and visible safety boundaries. It should feel like a thoughtful part of Intuition Box rather than a separate learning-management product.

### Aesthetic Direction

Premium editorial curriculum inside the existing Intuition Box dark theme. Reuse Google Sans Flex, mint brand tokens, dark tinted surfaces, the Logomark, PageHero, existing spacing, and restrained motion. Semantic graph lines and nodes are the memorable visual signature. Avoid generic dashboard card grids, glassmorphism, gamified LMS conventions, neon developer aesthetics, and a separate microsite language.

### Design Principles

1. Lead users to the aha moment quickly: select an idea, model its atoms and triple, then see the product surface it enables.
2. Teach one protocol concept at a time and keep advanced detail contextual.
3. Make Testnet, tTRUST, read-only actions, and simulated writes unmistakable.
4. Use editorial hierarchy and graph structure instead of excessive containers or decorative cards.
5. Preserve every core action across mobile, tablet, desktop, keyboard, touch, zoom, and reduced-motion contexts.
